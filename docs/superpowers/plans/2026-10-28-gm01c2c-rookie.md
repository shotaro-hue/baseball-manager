## Review Focus

- Stale duplicate draft records must not replace the validated acquiring club or class.
- A controlled rookie joining at the 70-place boundary consumes its existing reservation once.
- Development joining at 70 controlled players preserves flags and creates no controlled place/floor.
- Decline removes both accepted capacity and actual funding; unrelated holds/history remain intact in deficit.
- Changed ownership, class, phase/date or missing future budgets must reject the whole candidate.
