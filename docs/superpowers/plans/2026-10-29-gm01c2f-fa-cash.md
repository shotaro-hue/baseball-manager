# GM-01C-2F FA cash compensation implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans, with TDD per task and one independent final source review. User authorized implementation through PR; do not repeat approval.

**Goal:** A/B FA cash liability stays reserved after acceptance and settles only on the former club's choice.
**Architecture:** Optional immutable basis on market entries; a compensation obligation in the spending plan changes from pending reserve to paired one-off settlement. Reuse quote/apply, accepted proposal/ledger identity and spring joining.
**Tech Stack:** Existing JavaScript/Vitest/Vite; no dependency or CI changes.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-gm01c2f-fa-cash-design.md`

## Review focus

- Dropping pending/resolved compensation state or either cost leg must never erase an accepted liability.
- Old-club receipt must not be booked before acceptance/selection or fabricate new annual funds.
- Deficit can receive compensation or settle an existing obligation, but cannot mask unrelated new spending.
- Spring join, later trades, zero/type-colliding IDs and JSON replay remain safe.
- Missing basis remains cancellable and blocked at acceptance; stale dates, basis changes and wrong club selection fail without mutation.
